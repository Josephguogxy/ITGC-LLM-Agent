THE CENTRAL IDEA

The LLM does not replace the optimizer. It organizes the operating loop around it.

Conventional methods solve isolated optimization tasks. Real grids need persistent coordination: translating goals into constraints, linking long-term plans to rolling dispatch, checking safety before execution, and recovering when conditions change. ITGC makes those steps one continuous, auditable process.

ARCHITECTURE

Three layers. One continuous loop.

Semantic reasoning stays separated from numerical optimization, while explicit bridge variables connect planning decisions to local operation.
